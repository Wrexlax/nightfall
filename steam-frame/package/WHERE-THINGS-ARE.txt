Nightfall for Steam Frame - where it keeps its files

Everything is inside your home folder on the headset (~ below; on the Frame
that is /home/steamos). Nothing is installed system-wide.

THE APP
  ~/Games/NightfallFrame/
      The program, its launcher (nightfall.sh), the installer and
      uninstaller, the licences and this file. About 180 MB.
  ~/Games/NightfallFrame/depth_models/
      The three depth models for AI 3D made on the headset itself (41 MB
      together). They come with the package; nothing is downloaded.
  ~/Games/NightfallFrame/logs/
      One log per launch (the newest 10 are kept). See LOGS below.
  Steam library: "Nightfall (Steam Frame)", under Non-Steam games
      A shortcut that starts nightfall.sh. Steam keeps it and its artwork
      in its own files.

VOICE TYPING (the speech models)
  ~/.local/share/nightfall/whisper/
      ggml-base.en.bin     142 MB   Speech Model: Base
      ggml-small.en.bin    466 MB   Speech Model: Small
      ggml-base.bin        142 MB   the same two for other languages than
      ggml-small.bin       466 MB   English (only if you chose them)
      Downloaded once by the installer from whisper.cpp's model page on
      huggingface.co and checked before use. Kept outside the app's folder
      so an update doesn't download them again. Delete the folder to get
      the space back; voice typing is then off until the installer is run
      again. What you say is turned into text on the headset and is never
      sent anywhere or saved.

YOUR SETTINGS AND PAIRINGS
  ~/.local/share/NightfallFrame/
      (Before build 8u: ~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/Nightfall/, which
      the original Nightfall's Steam Frame build uses as well. Your settings
      were copied from there once; that folder is left as it was.)
      app_state.cfg            your settings
      host_state.cfg           stream settings and screen layouts, per PC
      last_connection.cfg      the PC you used last
      button_bindings.cfg, button_profiles.cfg
                               your buttons and saved profiles
      monitor_presets_custom.json
                               screen layouts you saved
      addons/nightfall-stream/config.ini, meteor_keys.cfg
                               your saved PCs and this headset's pairing
                               keys. Treat these like a password: with
                               them, another device could connect to your
                               PCs as this headset.
      Logs and Godot's drawing cache are here too.
      Delete the folder to start from scratch; each PC then has to be
      paired again.

LOGS
  Written only while Advanced Settings > Logging is On (off on a new
  install; see README.txt). With it off, the one thing kept is
  ~/Games/NightfallFrame/logs/last-run.txt: when the last run began, when
  it ended and its exit code.
  ~/Downloads/Nightfall-logs/
      The copy to attach to a message or post when asking for help. It is
      cleaned for sharing while it is written: network addresses, device
      and pairing ids, keys, PINs and names are masked (192.168.1.50 reads
      x.x.x.50). share_log.py in the app's folder is the cleaner, and
      lists everything it masks.
  ~/Games/NightfallFrame/logs/  and  ~/.local/share/NightfallFrame/logs/
      The untouched logs, for finding faults on this headset. They hold
      your PC's address on your home network and the ids the headset and
      PC use with each other. Nothing in them reaches anyone unless you
      send the file yourself.
  ~/Games/nightfall-install.log
      What the installer did the last time it ran.

OUTSIDE NIGHTFALL'S OWN FOLDERS
  SteamVR's settings (~/.config/openvr/config/steamvr.vrsettings)
      The installer adds video settings for Nightfall alone (resolution,
      refresh rate, motion smoothing off). You can change them in
      SteamVR's per-application video settings. They stay after an
      uninstall and do nothing without the app.
      Nightfall's own FPS setting changes one of them while it runs: the
      refresh rate, so the headset's display matches the stream.

REMOVING NIGHTFALL
  uninstall.sh removes the app, its Steam library entry and its logs. It
  asks separately about the speech models and about your settings and
  pairings, so you can keep them for a later install.
  By hand: remove the shortcut in Steam (Manage > Remove non-Steam game
  from your library) and delete the folders above.

ON YOUR PC
  Nothing from this package. Each PC you paired with lists this headset
  among its paired devices (in Sunshine, Apollo or Vibepollo's web page:
  PIN / Clients); remove it there if you want the PC to forget it.
  Nightfall Meteor, if you use it, is a separate program with its own
  uninstaller on the PC.
